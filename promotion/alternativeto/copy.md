# Sidequest

Website: https://sdqst.app

## Short description

A calm personal task manager with custom lanes, nested tasks, notes, and whiteboards. Designed with ADHD in mind.

## Description

Sidequest brings tasks, ideas, and everyday life onto one calm canvas. Capture thoughts in Inbox, focus on Today, and arrange custom lanes around your projects and interests.

Break tasks into smaller steps, collapse the details, and keep notes, sketches, and useful links close to the work. Keyboard shortcuts help you create, edit, complete, nest, and move tasks quickly, with drag and drop when you prefer it.

Board edits save locally and queue for sync when your connection drops. Connect GitHub issues to a lane, or use a compatible AI assistant through MCP to find and organise tasks.
