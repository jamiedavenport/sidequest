## Description

Sidequest is a personal task manager for work, side projects, and everyday life. Designed with ADHD in mind, it gives tasks, ideas, and their context a calm place to live.

Capture a thought in Inbox. Use Today to see what is due today. Create custom lanes for projects and interests, then break tasks into smaller steps with as many levels of nesting as you need. Collapse the details when you want the bigger picture.

Keep notes, links, and whiteboards attached to the task they belong to. Outline a release, sketch a process, or save the context you will need when you come back.

Create, edit, nest, move, and complete tasks from the keyboard, with drag and drop when you prefer it. Board edits save locally and queue for sync when your connection drops; connected devices receive updates as you work.

For builders, GitHub integration brings matching open issues into a lane. MCP lets a compatible AI assistant find tasks, capture ideas, and organise your board, with access you control.

Try Sidequest at https://sdqst.app. Feedback on the board, keyboard flow, and integrations is welcome.
